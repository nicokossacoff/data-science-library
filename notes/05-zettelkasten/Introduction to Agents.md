# Introduction to Agents

- **AI agents** are systems that use Large Language Models (LLM) to autonomously complete tasks on behalf of the user.
- Agents are aware of their environment and are capable of reasoning, planning, and using tools in order to achieve a task without human supervision.
- Their main features are **reasoning** (i.e., the ability to analyze data, draw conclusions and make decisiones) and **acting** (i.e., the ability to perform tasks based on a plan).
## ReAct Framework

- The main idea behind the **ReAct** framework is that there's a close relationship between **reasoning** and **action**.

> [...] This tight synergy between “acting” and “reasoning” allows humans to learn new tasks quickly and perform robust decision making or reasoning, even under previously unseen circumstances or facing information uncertainties.
